Our Story

BUILT DIFFERENT. WORN BY THE BOLD.

The Prodigal Son started as a small run of self-designed graphics printed for friends — no warehouse, no investors, just a refusal to blend in. Every drop since has been built the same way: heavyweight fabrics, in-house prints, and cuts that hold their shape wash after wash.

We're not chasing trends. We're building a wardrobe for people who left the crowd behind and came back with something to say. Streetwear made for the bold, designed to break the mold — shipped worldwide.
